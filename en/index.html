What it does

The tools that know your obligations are paid online services that keep your data. The free tools don't know your country's rules. Almanach does both.

Never miss a deadline

Returns, payments, mission ends and invoices to chase, in a calendar by month, week, list or agenda.

  • Reminders by e-mail or ntfy, with quiet hours.
  • An iCal feed for your calendar app.
  • Every deadline links to its official source.

Know how much to set aside

Almanach estimates the year's contributions and tax, month by month, and tracks thresholds: micro ceiling, VAT exemption.

A new contract coming? Type the amount and see what changes. Nothing is saved.

Invoice properly

Quotes, invoices, deposits and credit notes as PDFs, in French, English or German, with the legal mentions of your country and your client's.

  • Numbering, logo, colour, layout and font, your way.
  • Changing the numbering after the first invoice waits for 1 January, so the year's series stays unbroken.
  • Time not billed yet goes on an invoice in one click.

Stay in control of the rules

Each country's rules live in signed packs (Ed25519). A received update is never applied by itself: you read what changes, in plain words, then apply it or leave it for later.

Back to the shipped pack in one click.

And the rest

  • Your situation follows the calendar. Change status or country from a given date. Ending your activity keeps the last returns.
  • Bring your clients. CSV import from your previous tool, with a report of rejected lines before anything is imported.
  • Keep your data. Full JSON export, re-import into a fresh instance.
  • A well-kept account. Two-factor authentication (TOTP), revocable sessions, lockout after repeated failures.
  • Everywhere. Desktop, tablet, phone, light or dark theme, in French, English or German.

Install

You need Docker and Docker Compose. It runs on a server, a NAS or a Raspberry Pi.

git clone https://github.com/chteau/almanach.git
cd almanach
cp .env.example .env   # then fill in your settings
docker compose up -d

Then open http://localhost:8080. The setup wizard asks for the token printed in the logs (docker compose logs almanach), then creates the administrator account.

The variables (e-mail, packs, maintenance) are in the README.

Almanach helps you not forget anything and gives estimates. It is not tax or accounting advice: always check with your country's official sources, linked in the app.
